Memory Link Workflow (Like Console)

1. Select your SOURCE fixtures in the exact order you want them.
2. Press Ctrl+C to copy them into memory.
3. Clear your selection, then select your TARGET fixtures.
4. Press Ctrl+V. The sources will wrap/map to targets perfectly!
